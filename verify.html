Sentari offline verifier

Check a compliance report, then read it

Confirm that a report you were sent is genuine and has not been altered — then read the assessment. Everything happens on this computer: nothing is uploaded, and this page makes no network connections at all.

  1. Open the file you were sent — a .zip pack, or the .json files from one you extracted.
  2. This page re-checks, on this machine, that nothing in it has changed since it was signed.
  3. You read the assessment, and can print it for your file.
